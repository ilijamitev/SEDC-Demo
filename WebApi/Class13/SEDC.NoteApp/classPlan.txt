#################### INTRO ####################

	1. Koga i za sto imate upotrebeno logiranje ?
	2. Sto e logiranje ?
		=> del od kodot koj ni ovozmozuva kontinuirano sledenje na rabotenjeto na nasata aplikacija
	3. Nivoa na logiranje  
		1. Trace - lowest severity
		2. Debug - for debugging purposes during development
		3. *Information* - important insights into the application's behavior, such as startup and shutdown messages, significant application events, or successful operations
		4. Warning - Indicates a potential issue that does not prevent the application from functioning but should be investigated
		5. *Error* - Indicates a significant problem or error that needs attention
		6. Fatal - The highest severity level. Application termination
	4. Kade se moze da se logira ? => konzola, fajl, tabela vo baza
	5. Defaulten (vgraden) loger
	6. Biblioteki za logiranje (Log4net, NLog, Serilog)
